Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about IPAC’s work.

What is an NGO?

Non-governmental organizations (NGOs), also known in Brazil as civil society organizations (OSCs), are private, non-profit entities, independent from government, working across many fields of knowledge and social action.

Driven by a commitment to social, environmental, cultural or economic well-being, NGOs play a key role in building a fairer, more sustainable and more democratic society.

What are the essential features of an NGO?

NGOs are not government bodies and do not seek profit. Their resources come from donations, partnerships, services and other fundraising activities. They work for causes of public interest, such as human rights, environmental protection, education, health, culture and social and economic development.

In short, NGOs develop innovative solutions to complex social problems, testing new approaches and technologies that help improve public policy.

What is IPAC and what does it do?

IPAC is a non-profit organization dedicated to protecting, preserving and restoring the environment. Founded in 2004, its mission is to carry out work that protects the environment, promotes culture and environmental education and encourages volunteering. Learn more.

What is IPAC’s most recent project, and why does it matter?

IPAC is currently running Cultivando Água Boa Tangará, which aims to restore the springs and protected areas of the Queima Pé and Ararão rivers, essential to Tangará da Serra, and to secure the city’s water future. It also includes environmental education activities with students from public and private schools.

What is the ECA Ambiental?

It is the popular name of Brazilian Law No. 15,512 of September 22, 2026, which guarantees children and adolescents the right to nature, with absolute priority. Learn about the law and how it relates to IPAC’s work.

How can I support IPAC?

You can make a donation, buy products from the IPAC Store, share our work on social media or volunteer through the Contact page.
