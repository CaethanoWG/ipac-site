New Law No. 15,512 of September 22, 2026

ECA Ambiental: every child has the right to nature.

Brazil’s new federal law recognizes the right of children and adolescents to nature, with absolute priority. IPAC embraces this cause with a focus on environmental awareness: taking students to the springs, training educators and bringing families closer to water, soil and biodiversity.

  • Access to healthy natural areas
  • Free play in contact with nature
  • Nature-based education
  • Protecting and restoring nature

Meet IPAC

Technical knowledge serving nature and people.

Founded by a multidisciplinary group of professionals — lawyers, administrators, biologists, agronomists, teachers and technicians — IPAC carries out environmental protection, preservation and restoration work, as well as cultural and volunteering initiatives.

Since 2006, it has served on public councils such as the Mato Grosso State Water Resources Council, the Sepotuba River Basin Committee and the Municipal Environment Council.
