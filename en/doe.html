Why donate

The water that reaches your home starts at a spring.

Since 2004, IPAC has worked to protect and restore nature in Mato Grosso, Brazil. In 2021, restoring the headwaters of the Queima Pé River, Tangará da Serra’s main water source, showed what this work can achieve: the headwaters have not run dry since.

These results exist because there are people in the field — technicians, volunteers, educators and students — and resources to keep the work going. As a non-profit, IPAC depends on donations and partnerships to continue.

  • Environmental awareness

    Activities with schools and communities, in line with the ECA Ambiental.

  • Spring restoration

    Clearing spring outlets, contour lines, reforestation and groundwater recharge.

  • Long-term projects

    Monthly giving makes it possible to plan long-term work.

We report on how funds are used on the Transparency page. Amounts are in Brazilian reais (R$).
